# Skills that show — 2026-10-27

> Boundary: the measurements behind the human's request that the skills differ visibly
> ([impacts](../game/impacts.md) rule 2, [autopilot](../pilot/autopilot.md) rule 5,
> [run](../game/run.md) rule 3), with the props at twice life size. NOT covered: the deck criteria
> themselves (their tests are in [pins physics](../game/pins.md)); the earlier campaign on the
> 300 m lane is [pilot vs blind, 300 m](pilot-vs-blind-300m-20261027.md).

## What was measured, where

- Code: commit `3467546` («Make the skills show…»), on top of the twice-life-size props
  (`726b79b`) and the alley (`1ac9d70`).
- Environment: Vitest 3.2.7 with Babylon.js 9.32.0 `NullEngine` and Havok 1.3.14 WASM in Node
  26.0.0 for the deck sweep; headless Chromium 156 (Playwright 1.64.1, WebGL on SwiftShader) on the
  Vite 7.3.7 dev server for the campaigns; macOS 27.0.1, Apple arm64.
- Deck sweep: `simulateDeck` (the deck tests' harness) with the ball handed over at the pocket plus
  an offset `d`, rolling, no other error; a throw-away test, not committed.
- Campaigns: `npm run pilot -- --tracks all --skill <s> --runs 3` for perfect, good and sloppy, and
  `--blind`; three seeds per track (the track's, 1 and 2), `speedup=60`. 60 runs each.

At 9 m/s the curve is the same within one pin. A twice-life-size ball and deck strike anywhere
from 0.3 m left to 0.1 m right of the pocket; speed hardly matters.

**Campaign 1** (loss 0.4, recovery 0.5 m/s², aim ± 0.24 m random side, ignore good 0.25 / sloppy
0.55): pins perfect 10.0, good 9.4, sloppy 8.7 — sloppy still struck 8 of 15. **Campaign 2** (aim
± 0.5 m random side, sloppy 0.70): good 8.7, sloppy 8.3 with 2.7 impacts a run and 10 strikes in
15 — errors of a random side cancel out. **Campaign 3**, the values now in the spec (each impact

- Strikes: perfect 15 of 15, good 5, sloppy 0, blind 1. Zero pins: sloppy 6, blind 12.
- Speed at the hand-over: perfect 12.0, good 11.9, sloppy 11.2, blind 9.9 m/s (averages).
- Rolling time: perfect 40.0 s, good 40.0–43.5 s, sloppy 42.5–48.9 s, blind 41.5–55.3 s.
- All 60 runs reached the result, 0 console errors; browser and `simulateRun` agree on impacts in
  45 of 45 pilot runs. Peak active meshes 190 (budget 250), with the alley drawn.

## Reading

The three skills now end far apart — a strike, a handful of pins, almost nothing — and the run
itself shows why: one impact against two or three, a slower ball, a longer run. The lever was not
the size of the aim error but its direction: random-side errors cancel, so a sloppy run struck as
often as a good one until every impact of a run pushed the same way. Sloppy is now close to the
blind baseline in pins (2.5 against 1.7) while hitting far fewer obstacles (2.7 against 4.0); a
human may find it too harsh.

## Scope

Three seeds per track, one machine, one software renderer (frame times are not meaningful here).
The aim side is drawn from the track seed, so every run of a track misses to the same side. Not
tried: other speeds at the deck, entry angles, real GPUs.
